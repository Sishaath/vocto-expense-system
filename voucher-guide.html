How the Voucher System Works
Overview of the full approval workflow

The Vocto Voucher System is used for two purposes:
1. Advance Request — You need money for upcoming work and require approval before spending.
2. Reimbursement — You have already spent from your own pocket and need to be paid back.

Employee — Submitting a Voucher
How to submit an expense or advance request
Employee Dashboard
📸
Screenshot: Employee Dashboard
Shows My Vouchers list with status badges
1
Click "+ New Voucher" in the sidebar or top button
This opens the voucher submission form.
2
Fill in the voucher details
Voucher Title — Short description (e.g. "Client visit travel")
Category — Select the type (Travel, Meals, Office Supplies, etc.)
Amount (₹) — The amount you are requesting or claiming back
Expense Date — The date the expense was incurred
Vendor / Payee — Who was paid (e.g. hotel name, cab company)
Payment Mode — How it was paid (Cash, Company Card, UPI, etc.)
Description — Brief explanation of the purpose
3
Attach supporting documents
Upload your bill, receipt, or invoice. Supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG. Multiple files can be uploaded.
4
Click "Submit Voucher"
Your voucher is submitted and the Accounts team will be notified. You can track the status from your dashboard.
📸
Screenshot: Submit Voucher Form
vocto-expense-system.vercel.app/submit

Can I edit after submitting? Yes — but only while the status is PENDING. Once Accounts verifies it, the voucher is locked.

3
Accounts — Verifying Vouchers
Yogeshwari · yogeshwari@voctotechnologies.com
Accounts Dashboard
📸
Screenshot: Accounts Dashboard
Shows "Vouchers — Needs Verification" section
1
Review the voucher
New submissions appear under Vouchers — Needs Verification. Click the row to open the detail panel and review all information.
2
Check the attachment
Click View to open the receipt or bill. Verify the amount matches and the document is valid.
3a
Click "✓ Verify" if everything is correct
The voucher moves to MD for approval. MD will be notified automatically.
3b
Click "✕ Reject" if there is an issue
A dialog will appear asking for the rejection reason. Enter a clear reason so the employee knows what to fix. The employee will be notified.
4
Release payment once MD approves
Approved vouchers appear under Ready for Payment. Click Release Payment after processing the bank transfer. The employee will be notified.
📸
Screenshot: Ready for Payment section
Shows "Release Payment" button after MD approval
4
MD — Approving Vouchers
RRK · rrk@voctotechnologies.com
MD Dashboard
📸
Screenshot: MD Dashboard
Shows "Vouchers Awaiting Your Approval" section
1
Review verified vouchers
Vouchers verified by Accounts appear under Vouchers Awaiting Your Approval. Click the row to see full details including category, amount, and attachment.
2a
Click "✓ Approve" to approve
The voucher moves to Ready for Payment in the Accounts dashboard. Accounts will be notified to release the payment.
2b
Click "✕ Reject" to reject with reason
Enter a reason. The employee will be notified of the rejection and the reason provided.
3
View history under "Recently Processed"
All vouchers you have approved or rejected are visible under the Recently Processed section for your reference.

Stat cards are clickable. Click the "Awaiting Approval" card on the dashboard to filter and see only those vouchers. Click again to go back to all.

Quick Reference
Things to remember when using the system
Do's
✓
Always attach the original bill or receipt
✓
Use a clear voucher title so accounts team understands
✓
Submit on the same day or within 3 days of expense
✓
Check the Rejected section if you don't see your voucher move forward
Don'ts
✕
Don't submit without attaching the bill
✕
Don't enter incorrect amounts — it will be rejected
✕
Don't share your login credentials with anyone
✕
Don't wait to ask — use the Comments section in the voucher detail if you have questions
