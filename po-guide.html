What is a Purchase Order?
Overview of the PO workflow

A Purchase Order (PO) is a formal document raised when Vocto Technologies needs to purchase goods or services from a vendor. It must be approved before any commitment is made to the vendor.

The PO includes vendor details, line items (products/services), quantities, rates, GST, and the total amount.

Who can create a PO? Any user who is logged in — Employee, Accounts, or MD. The "+ Create PO" option is available in the sidebar on every screen.

1
Creating a Purchase Order
Any user — Employee, Accounts or MD
📸
Screenshot: Create PO Form
vocto-expense-system.vercel.app/po/create
1
Click "+ Create PO" in the sidebar
Available on any page. Opens the full PO creation form.
2
Enter vendor details
Enter the vendor's GSTIN and click Fetch to auto-fill their name, address and state.
Fill in vendor email, phone if available.
3
Add line items
For each item/service enter:
Description — What is being purchased
HSN Code — Product/service tax code
Qty — Quantity
Unit Price (₹) — Rate per unit
GST % — 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%
The amount is calculated automatically.
4
Review totals
The system automatically calculates Subtotal, CGST/SGST (or IGST for interstate), and Grand Total. The amount in words is also generated.
5
Save as Draft or Submit
Save as Draft — Saves without submitting. You can come back and edit later.
Submit for Approval — Sends the PO to Accounts for verification. Once submitted, it cannot be edited.

Important: Once you click "Submit for Approval", the PO is locked. Make sure all details are correct before submitting.

2
Viewing a Purchase Order
PDF-ready PO view with all GST details
📸
Screenshot: PO View / Print Page
vocto-expense-system.vercel.app/po/view/[id]
1
Click "View" next to any PO
Opens the full formatted PO document with company header, vendor details, line items, GST breakdown and terms.
2
Print or save as PDF
Click the Print / Save PDF button on the PO view page. Use Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) and select "Save as PDF" to get a clean print-ready document to share with the vendor.

GST is automatically handled. If your vendor is in Tamil Nadu (same state as Vocto), the PO will show CGST + SGST. If the vendor is in another state, it will show IGST automatically.

1
Open the Accounts Dashboard
Submitted POs appear at the top under Purchase Orders — Needs Verification.
2
Click "View" to review the PO
Opens the full formatted PO. Review vendor details, line items, amounts, and GST to ensure everything is correct.
3
Click "✓ Verify" to send to MD
The PO moves to the MD dashboard for final approval. MD will be notified.
